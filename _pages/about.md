I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the [University of California, Davis](https://economics.ucdavis.edu/), currently in my fourth year. My research lies at the intersection of **energy and environmental economics** and **public finance**, with a focus on how taxes, subsidies, and prices shape consumer behavior in energy markets.

My job market paper studies what retail gasoline prices can — and cannot — tell us about cross-border shopping, using evidence from Argentina.

Before coming to Davis, I completed my MA and BA in Economics at the [Universidad Nacional de La Plata (UNLP)](https://www.econo.unlp.edu.ar/) in Argentina, where I was awarded the *Manuel Belgrano* prize for top undergraduate GPA and the prize for best MA thesis.

In 2025 I was named a [National Tax Association Climate Tax Fellow](https://ntanet.org/2025/08/2025-national-tax-association-climate-tax-fellowship-application/).
